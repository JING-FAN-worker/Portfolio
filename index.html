    Hi, I’m Jing, also known as Jane. I’m a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at Aalto University in Finland. My research explores how large language models can support programming education, with a focus on understanding students’ knowledge and providing personalized feedback.

    My background combines software engineering, machine learning, and teaching. Before joining Aalto, I completed my bachelor’s and master’s degrees at LUT University, where I also worked in research and supported students in programming, mathematics, and statistics.

    Beyond research, I enjoy bringing people together. I serve on the AaltoAI board as Head of Partnership and Growth. Volunteering is also part of my life: I have been involved in animal rescue since 2016 and have participated in environmental and community initiatives.
